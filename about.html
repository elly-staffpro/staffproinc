The PEO Model

How the PEO Model Works

As a PEO, StaffPro takes on your HR, payroll, and compliance obligations — handling the administrative and legal responsibilities of employment while you stay in charge of your employees' day-to-day work and all business decisions.

You Stay in Control

You direct your employees' work, make all hiring and firing decisions, and run your business your way. StaffPro only steps in on the administrative and legal employment obligations.

We Take On the Liability

StaffPro files payroll taxes under our EIN, manages workers' compensation, and assumes responsibility for HR compliance obligations — reducing your risk exposure significantly.

Your Employees Benefit

Your team gains access to comprehensive benefits packages — medical, dental, 401(k), and more — typically only available to much larger employers.

You Focus on Growth

With payroll, HR, benefits, and compliance off your plate, you can redirect your time and energy toward growing your business and serving your customers.

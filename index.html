What We Do

Everything Your Business Needs to Manage Its People

From your first hire to your hundredth, StaffPro provides the infrastructure, expertise, and tools to keep your workforce running smoothly.

Payroll Processing

Accurate, on-time payroll every cycle — including tax calculations, direct deposit, and year-end reporting.

  • Weekly, bi-weekly & semi-monthly cycles
  • Direct deposit & live check options
  • Tax filing & remittance
  • W-2 preparation & distribution

HR Administration

Expert HR support that keeps your policies current, your onboarding smooth, and your employees informed.

  • Employee handbook consulting & support
  • New hire onboarding via PayPlus
  • HR policy & compliance guidance
  • Anti-harassment training

Employee Benefits

Give your employees access to large-group benefits at rates small businesses couldn't achieve on their own.

  • Medical, dental & vision insurance
  • 401(k) plan administration
  • Life & disability insurance
  • Section 125 / cafeteria plans

Workers' Compensation

Comprehensive NY workers' compensation coverage with dedicated claims management and liability oversight.

  • Workers' compensation policy management
  • Claims filing assistance
  • Return-to-work coordination
  • Annual premium audits

Background Screening

Make confident hiring decisions with thorough pre-employment background checks and eligibility verification.

  • Criminal background checks
  • I-9 & E-Verify guidance
  • Motor vehicle record checks
  • Reference verification

Tax & Compliance

Stay ahead of complex employment tax obligations with expert filing, reporting, and regulatory monitoring.

  • Federal, state & local tax filing
  • NYS unemployment insurance
  • ACA reporting
  • Paid Family Leave compliance

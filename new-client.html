    Workers' compensation application

    We use your answers to fill in the workers' compensation application (ACORD 130) that we send to the insurance company for you.

    Before you start
    • Nothing on this page is required. Fill in what you can and leave the rest blank. We will follow up on anything missing.
    • Your business name, address, EIN and work locations are already filled in from the earlier steps.
    • Estimates are fine. Use your best guess for the next 12 months.
    • You do not need rates or premiums. The insurance company works those out.
    • We will send you the finished application to review and sign.
    About the business
    Enter a number.
    A few plain sentences. For example: a store (what you sell, who your customers are, whether you deliver); a contractor (the type of work, and what you hire subcontractors for); a manufacturer (materials, products, equipment); a service business (the service, and where the work is done).
    Employees by type of work

    Add one line for each type of work your employees do.

    • Location: which work location the employees are at. The numbers match the Work locations step.
    • Class code: the workers' compensation code for that work, if you know it. It is on your current policy. Leave it blank if you are not sure.
    • Duties: a short, clear description of the work, such as "Office clerical", "Cashiers and stock clerks" or "Delivery drivers".
    • Employees: about how many full-time and part-time employees do this work.
    • Annual wages: the estimated total yearly wages for everyone on that line.
